Chapter 00

What is MyLib?

MyLib is a personal library manager built as a Progressive Web App. It is designed for readers who want more than a spreadsheet — a place where your collection, your reading journey, your notes, and your reading community all live together.

MyLib runs in your browser, installs like a native app, works offline, and syncs your library to the cloud so your books follow you across every device.

📚
A real catalog Titles, authors, genres, tags, ISBNs, covers, prices, purchase dates, copy types, and ownership.
📈
Reading insights Charts and statistics that reveal the shape of your reading life.
🤖
AI Librarian Ask questions about your own shelves and get personalized recommendations.
👥
Collaboration Share libraries, transfer books, and read alongside people you trust.
💬
Community Reviews, likes, comments, and reader profiles from around the world.
📶
Offline-first Catalog books on a plane. Everything syncs when you reconnect.
💡
Who is MyLib for? Casual readers tracking a growing shelf, collectors managing hundreds of volumes, book clubs, families sharing a library, and anyone who has ever bought a book they already owned.

MyLib uses Google Sign-In. There is no password to remember and no separate signup form — your account is created automatically the first time you sign in.

01

Open MyLib and click Continue with Google on the landing page.

02

Choose the Google account you want to use. MyLib requests only basic profile information.

03

If you have signed in before, you land straight in your library. If not, your account is created automatically and you start with an empty shelf.

✅
On first sign-in MyLib creates a user profile document containing your email, display name, join date, and library counters. You can edit the display name, phone number, and address later in Settings → Profile.

Chapter 03

Adding Books

There are three ways to add a book to MyLib: scan a barcode, fill in a form manually, or let AI Magic Fill do the heavy lifting. All three end up in the same place.

Method 1 · Scan a barcode or QR code

The scanner uses your device camera and the html5-qrcode library. It reads ISBNs on the back of most printed books.

01

Open Add Book and tap Scan Barcode / QR.

02

If you have collaborators who allow it, MyLib first asks which library the book should go into.

03

Point the camera at the barcode. Hold steady — scanning happens automatically at 20 frames per second.

04

MyLib looks the ISBN up on Google Books first, then falls back to Open Library for missing covers or data.

05

Review the result. If nothing is found, MyLib opens the manual form pre-filled with the scanned code.

Lookup pipeline

Barcode decoded → 9780441013593
Google Books isbn: query
Open Library fallback (covers)
Smart text parser (Title: / Author: / ISBN:)
Approval modal → Save

Method 2 · Add manually

The manual form is the most complete way to catalog a book. It auto-saves a draft as you type so you never lose work if you close the tab.

📝
Title & Author Both required. The author field is a searchable dropdown that suggests authors already in your library.
🏷️
Genres (multi-select) Tap popular genre pills or type a custom one. Genres appear as removable chips.
🔖
Tags Free-form comma-separated keywords for cross-cutting themes like "space", "reread", "gift".
🖼️
Cover URL & ISBN Paste any image URL. If a scanned code exists it pre-fills the ISBN field.
📄
Description A live character counter appears below. Descriptions power AI summaries.
💵
Price & Purchase date Price uses your selected currency. Date feeds the date-range filters.
📦
Copy type New Copy · Old Copy · Gifted. Choosing Gifted reveals a "Gifted By" field.
💫
Wishlist toggle Mark the book as a wishlist item instead of adding it to the main library.
🏛️
Destination library Add to your own library, or to a collaborator's if they've granted permission.
💾
Draft recovery: if you close the manual form with unsaved content, MyLib stores a draft in your browser's session storage. The next time you open the form it asks whether you'd like to restore it. The draft is cleared when you save or explicitly close the form.

Method 3 · AI Magic Fill

Type just a title (or an ISBN) and press the ⚡ Magic Fill button. The AI Librarian returns a full metadata record.

⚡
What it fills Title, author, genres, tags, ISBN, price, and a professional description — all in one request.
🔒
Non-destructive Magic Fill only populates empty fields. Anything you've already typed stays untouched.

Duplicate detection

MyLib checks every new book against the target library before writing it.

🔍
Match rules First by normalised ISBN (digits and X only). If no ISBN, by exact title + author, case-insensitive.
♻️
On match MyLib asks whether to update the existing record instead. Nothing is deleted — your reading status and notes are preserved.

Adding finished books directly

From the My Books → Finished sub-tab, the Add a finished book button offers two paths: pick an existing book from your library and mark it finished, or add a brand-new record that is excluded from the main library view (perfect for books you read on loan or borrowed from a friend).

Chapter 04

Organizing Your Library

A well-organized library is a usable library. MyLib gives you several layers of structure so that a shelf of 20 books and a shelf of 2,000 books both stay navigable.

Genres

Genres are the primary axis of organization. A book can belong to multiple genres, and genres are shared across your whole library — so renaming "Sci-Fi" to "Science Fiction" in Settings updates every book at once.

Tags

Tags are free-form and personal. Where genres are broad and standardized, tags are specific: beach-read, reread-2026, borrowed-from-Sam, unfinished-series. Click any tag chip on a book card to filter by it instantly.

Copy type

🆕
New Copy Bought new. The default value.
📖
Old Copy Second-hand, inherited, or well-loved.
🎁
Gifted Received as a gift. Prompts for the giver's name.

Ownership & borrowed books

Every book has an owner. In a shared library, you'll see books owned by your collaborators in a separate section headed "From [Name]'s Library". A book currently lent out shows a Borrowed badge and is tracked in the Currently Borrowed list in Insights.

View modes and density

🔲
Grid The default. Rich cards with cover, genres, tags, rating, and price.
🔳
Compact Six-column cover grid. Best for browsing large collections quickly.
📋
List One book per row with inline metadata. Best for scanning text.
📐
Density Relaxed, Normal, or Tight padding — independent of view mode.

Bulk actions

Hover any book card and tick the checkbox in the top-left corner. A bulk-actions bar appears at the top of the library.

🏷️
Change genre Pick a genre from the dropdown to apply it to every selected book.
🔁
Transfer Move ownership of the selected books to an active collaborator.
🗑️
Delete Remove the selected books. An Undo link appears in the toast for 10 seconds.
⚠️
Transfers are permanent. When you transfer a book to a collaborator, you lose ownership immediately. There is no undo for transfers — only deletes can be undone.

Chapter 05

Search & Filters

MyLib's search box searches across title, author, genres, tags, and ISBN simultaneously. Filters let you narrow the results further.

Smart search

The search bar matches against a pre-built index string on every book. Because of this, typing a genre name, a tag, or an author surname all work without switching modes.

💡
"Did you mean…?" If your search returns nothing, MyLib runs a fuzzy similarity check against titles and authors and suggests the closest match.
🕘
Recent searches Your last five searches appear as chips under the search bar. Press Enter to save a search.
🤖
AI Smart Search When a normal search finds nothing, an AI Smart Search button appears. It understands natural language.
⌨️
Keyboard shortcuts Press / anywhere to jump straight into the search box. Press Esc to clear.

Standard filters

📚
Genre All genres or one specific genre.
🔖
Status All · Want to Read · Reading · Finished.
✍️
Author Every author currently in your library.
🔃
Sort Recently added, oldest, title A–Z, author, borrowed to others.

Advanced filters

Open the Filters button next to the search bar for the full panel.

⭐
Star rating Exactly 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 stars. Uses your rating first, then the book's average.
💰
Price range Minimum and maximum, in your selected currency.
📅
Date range Filter by purchase date — useful for "books I bought this year".

On mobile, the advanced filter panel also exposes sort, genre, status, and author, so everything is reachable in one modal.

🧩
Active filters appear as chips below the search bar with an × to remove them individually. When more than one filter is active, a Clear All button also appears. A small blue dot on the Filters button tells you filters are applied.

Chapter 06

Book Details & Reading Tracking

Click any book card to open the full details view. This is where all reading tracking happens — status, progress, rating, review, highlights, and borrowing.

Reading status

🔖
Want to Read The default for every new book. Grey badge.
📖
Reading Blue badge with a live progress percentage. Unlocks the reading session timer.
🎉
Finished Emerald badge. Triggers a confetti celebration when you set it.

Progress tracking

A book marked as Reading shows a progress bar on its card. You can update it in two places:

A

In the details modal, type an exact percentage into the progress field.

B

On the book card, hover the progress bar and press the + button to bump it by 5% instantly. At 100% you get confetti.

Rating & review

Inside the details modal you'll find a rating dropdown (0–5 stars) and a review textarea. Press Save Review to persist both. The book's average rating is recalculated across every user who has rated it, and the average appears on the card.

Highlights & quotes

Highlights are shared across everyone with access to the book. Each one stores the quote text, an optional page number, the author, and a timestamp.

✏️
Add Type the quote, optionally add a page number, press Enter or the + button.
📋
Copy Copies the quote formatted as "text" — Title, Author, ready to paste.
🗑️
Delete Only available for highlights you added yourself.

Reading session timer

When a book is marked Reading, a session timer appears in the details modal. Press Start Session and the timer counts up in MM:SS. Press Stop Session to end it — a toast tells you how many minutes you read. The timer keeps running if you close and reopen the modal.

⚡

📤

Finished Books

Your reading history

  • · An Add a finished book button for books not in your shelf.

Books you want next

Chapter 09

AI Librarian

The AI Librarian is a context-aware assistant that knows your shelves. It can recommend what to read next, analyze your collection, generate a reading roadmap, and fill in book metadata — all using an AI provider you control.

🔑
You need your own API key. MyLib does not ship with a bundled AI key. Add one in Settings → AI Librarian. Your key is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never sent anywhere except directly to the AI provider.

Supported providers

🔷
Google Gemini Default provider

A dropdown of models including Gemma 3 (12B, 4B, 1B, 27B), Gemini 2.5 Flash / Flash-Lite, and Gemini 3 previews. MyLib automatically retries with a fallback model if your first choice fails.

Get a key at Google AI Studio.

⚡
Groq Low-latency option

Uses openai/gpt-oss-20b for very fast responses. Great if you ask lots of short questions.

Get a key at Groq Console.

Language support

Choose a preferred language and the AI will respond in it — while keeping book titles, author names, genres, and tags in English so your catalog stays consistent.

English Bengali Spanish French German Arabic Hindi

The chat

Open the chat with the floating 🤖 button or any "Ask AI" button. The chat keeps conversational history for the session and shows a shimmering skeleton while it thinks.

⚡
Quick actions 📚 Recommend next book · 📊 Analyze my library · 🧠 Improve my collection · 📝 Summarize my library.
🔊
Read aloud Every AI reply has a speaker button that reads it back using your device's text to speech.
🧠
Context-aware The AI receives a compressed summary of your finished and unread books, plus your top genres.
🗑️
Clear chat The trash icon resets the conversation without touching your library.

AI-powered library tools

🔬
Library Analysis In Insights, press Analyze Library for a full report: Collection DNA, Reading Velocity, Missing Dimensions, and 5 curated recommendations. The result is saved so you can revisit it.
🗺️
Reading Roadmap A three-month plan: Month 1 Build Foundation, Month 2 Deep Dive, Month 3 Expand Horizons — prioritising books already on your wishlist.
🧹
Metadata Fixer Scans your library for missing descriptions, missing tags, spelling errors, and inconsistent genres. Shows you every proposed change for approval before applying.
✨
Magic Fill Inside the manual add form. Populates empty metadata fields from just a title or ISBN.
🧾
Token efficiency: MyLib compresses your library into a compact structure before sending it to the AI — abbreviated keys, limited history (last 50 finished books, max 50 unread). This keeps requests fast and cheap.

Chapter 11

Collaboration

Collaboration lets you connect your library to someone else's. Partners can see each other's collections, transfer books, and appear in each other's activity feeds.

Sending a request

01

Go to Settings → Library Sharing.

02

Type the collaborator's email address and press Add.

03

MyLib looks up the user. If they haven't signed in to MyLib before, you'll be told they don't exist yet.

04

A pending request appears in your list, labelled "Collaboration request sent". You can cancel it at any time.

Accepting a request

Incoming requests appear in two places: Settings → Library Sharing and Activity. Press Accept to create the partnership, or Reject to decline.

What a partnership unlocks

👀
Shared visibility Their books appear in your Library under "From [Name]'s Library", and yours appear in theirs.
🔁
Book transfers Send ownership of single books or bulk selections to a partner.
✍️
Shared reviews You can see your partner's rating and comments on any book you both have.
📰
Shared activity Both libraries' activity appears in your Activity tab.
➕
Add to their library If they grant the Can add books permission, you can add books directly to their shelf.
🏆
Leaderboard My Books shows a collaborator leaderboard, with a "Best reader" badge for the top finisher.

Permissions & unfollowing

Each partnership has two independent controls:

✅
Can add books A checkbox on each partnership row. When enabled, that partner can add books directly into your library.
🔇
Unfollow / Rejoin Unfollowing hides their books from your library without ending the partnership. You can rejoin at any time. You can't unfollow a partner who has unfollowed you.
🤝
Collaborate deliberately. Sharing a library is a trust relationship. Only connect with people you'd be comfortable lending a physical book to.

Chapter 12

Borrowing & Book Requests

MyLib tracks two sides of lending: books you lend out, and books you want to borrow from someone else.

Lending a book out

01

Open the book's details modal.

02

In the Borrowing section, type the borrower's name — or leave it blank to record it as borrowed by you.

03

Press Borrow. The book gets a Borrowed badge and appears in Insights → Currently Borrowed.

04

When it comes back, press Return. The loan is closed and archived in the book's borrow history.

Requesting a book from a collaborator

This is a lightweight, consent-first workflow. Nothing changes hands until the owner agrees.

The requester

1. Search for a collaborator by email in Explore.
2. Expand any available book to see its details.
3. Press Request Book. The button then shows Request Pending.
4. Your phone number and address from your profile are included so the owner can arrange the hand-off.

The owner

1. The request appears in Activity → Pending Book Requests.
2. You see the requester's name, phone, and address.
3. Press Accept — the book is automatically marked as borrowed to that person, with the loan date recorded.
4. Or press Reject — the requester is notified via their activity feed.
🚫
Guardrails: you can't request your own book, you can't request a book that's already borrowed, and MyLib prevents duplicate pending requests for the same title.

Chapter 14

Import, Export & Backup

Your library belongs to you. MyLib gives you four ways to move data in and out, and every import is reversible.

The four formats

📥
Import CSV Accepts columns: Title, Author, Genre / Category, ISBN, Notes / Comments, Price, Date Added, Status. Missing genres are auto-guessed from the title.
📤
Export CSV A lightweight four-column file: Title, Author, Category, Tags. Good for spreadsheets.
🗂️
Import JSON The full-fidelity format. Restores covers, descriptions, prices, ratings, statuses, progress, wishlist flags, highlights, and community reviews.
📦
Export JSON A complete backup of your library including reading status, highlights, and every review you've written.

How imports behave

01

Duplicates are merged, not duplicated. If a book already exists (by ISBN or title + author), MyLib updates the existing record.

02

Highlights are combined. On JSON import, existing highlights and imported highlights are merged by text so nothing is lost.

03

Reviews are de-duplicated. A review is only imported if you don't already have one with identical text for that title.

04

Everything is undoable. After a CSV import, an Undo link appears in the toast for ten seconds. It deletes newly added books and restores updated ones to their previous state.

🗓️
Back up regularly. Export JSON once a month and keep the file somewhere safe. It's the only format that preserves your full reading history, highlights, and reviews.

Managing metadata in bulk

In Settings → Library Data you'll find lists of every author and genre in your library. Each entry has two actions:

✏️
Rename Change the name everywhere it appears, across every book you own.
🗑️
Remove Strip a genre or author from every book. Books left without a genre fall back to "Other"; authors fall back to "Unknown".

Chapter 16

Offline, Sync & PWA

MyLib is built offline-first. Your library loads from a local cache, then reconciles with the cloud when a connection is available.

Installing as an app

01

Open MyLib in Chrome, Edge, or a Chromium-based browser on Android or desktop.

02

Go to Settings → Notifications. If your browser supports installation, an Install App button appears.

03

Press it and confirm. MyLib opens in its own window, with its own icon on your home screen or dock.

How offline works

📥
Reads come from cache Firestore's offline persistence means your library is available without a connection.
✍️
Writes are queued Adding a book, updating a status, or writing a review works offline. Changes are queued locally.
🔄
Sync is automatic When your connection returns, queued writes flush to the cloud automatically — no action required.

App updates

MyLib registers a service worker. When a new version is deployed, a toast appears at the bottom of the screen: "New version available — Refresh". Pressing it activates the update and reloads the app.

Connectivity indicator

The pill at the top of the main area always tells you where you stand:

Live — connected, cloud sync active
Offline — local changes will sync when reconnected

Tips & Best Practices

🏷️
Keep genres broad, tags specific Use genres for the shelf you'd find a book on. Use tags for everything personal — mood, project, who lent it to you.
📅
Always fill purchase date It powers the date-range filter and the "Oldest First" sort. It's the one field people forget and later miss.
⭐
Rate as you finish Ratings feed the distribution chart and sharpen the AI's recommendations. Even a quick 3-star helps.
📝
Write one sentence per book You'll forget the plot in six months but remember how it made you feel. Capture that.
📦
Use copy types honestly Marking a book as Gifted with the giver's name turns your library into a record of generosity.
🎯
Set a realistic yearly goal Better to beat 12 than abandon 100. You can change it in Settings any time.
🧹
Run Metadata Fixer monthly It catches typos, missing descriptions, and inconsistent genre names before they compound.
💾
Export JSON every month Ten seconds of effort, and you'll never lose years of reading history.
👥
Collaborate with intention Start with one person you actually share books with. The shared activity feed is more meaningful with a small circle.
🤖
Ask the AI specific questions "Which of my unread books are under 300 pages?" beats "recommend something." Specific questions get specific answers.

Chapter 19

Troubleshooting

● My library isn't loading

Check the connectivity pill. If it says Offline, your cached data should still appear. If the screen stays on the skeleton loader, try refreshing the page. A hard refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R) clears a stale service worker.

● Changes aren't saving

You're probably offline. Queued writes sync automatically when the connection returns. If the pill says Live and things still aren't saving, sign out and back in to refresh your authentication token.

● The scanner won't open

Camera access requires HTTPS and explicit permission. Check your browser's site settings and make sure camera access is allowed. On iOS Safari, camera access works best when MyLib is added to the home screen.

● AI says "invalid API key"

Re-copy the key from your provider's dashboard, paste it into Settings → AI Librarian, and press Save AI Settings. Make sure you selected the right provider — a Gemini key won't work with Groq.

● AI says "rate limit reached"

You've hit your provider's free-tier quota. Wait a few minutes, or switch to a different provider. Repeated identical questions are served from a small in-session cache, so rephrasing may help.

● A collaborator's books aren't showing

Check three things: the partnership request was accepted (not pending), neither of you has pressed Unfollow, and you're both online to sync. Their books appear under a separate heading below your own.

● I accidentally deleted a book

Press the Undo link in the toast immediately — it's available for ten seconds and restores both the book and your reading status. After ten seconds, the deletion is permanent unless you have a JSON backup.

● The app shows an error toast

MyLib has a global error boundary. It reassures you that your data is safe and offers a Reload button. Reloading re-establishes listeners without losing anything that was already saved.

● A book was added twice

Delete the duplicate from the details modal. To avoid it in future, make sure the ISBN field is filled — ISBN matching is more reliable than title matching, and it catches editions with slightly different titles.

Chapter 20

Privacy & Your Data

A library is personal. Here's exactly what MyLib stores, where it lives, and who can see it.

Where things are stored

☁️
Cloud (Firebase) Your books, reading statuses, reviews, highlights, profile, and partnerships. Protected by security rules that scope each document to its owner.
💻
Local (browser) Theme, accent colour, currency, reading goal, view preferences, AI provider and key, and your recent searches.
🗂️
Session storage Your unsaved manual-entry draft. Cleared when you save or close the form, and gone when you close the tab.

What others can see

Data Who can see it
Public reviews & posts Anyone using MyLib
Display name, bio, book counts Anyone who searches for your email
Your book list & library Accepted collaborators only
Your ratings & comments on shared books Owners and collaborators of that book
Your wishlist Only you — always private, even in shared libraries
Phone number & address Only shared when you request a book, and only with the book's owner
Your AI API key Never leaves your browser except to call your chosen AI provider directly
🔒
On AI keys: your key is stored in local storage and sent only to the provider you selected. It is never transmitted to MyLib's servers, because MyLib has no servers — it's a client-side application talking directly to Firebase and your AI provider.

Account deletion

Account deletion is not currently available from within MyLib. If you need your data removed, export a JSON backup first and then contact the developer through the link in the footer.
