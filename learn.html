Chapter One

Adding Books to Your Library

Cataloging your sanctuary is elegant and seamless. Tap the '+' button in the main library screen to open manual entry or barcode scanners. You can scan barcodes directly with your device's camera, fetch metadata dynamically from Google Books, or import full collections from CSV/JSON.

Chapter Two

Organization & Interactive Filtering

Keep your digital sanctuary perfectly aligned. Organize files by custom tags, multi-genre chips, reading statuses, or financial price values. The advanced real-time filters let you navigate hundreds of files smoothly, finding exact matches by ratings or purchase date range instantly.

Chapter Three

Collaborators & Shared Libraries

MyLib is social. Add trusted collaborators via their emails in Settings. Once your partner request is accepted, your libraries synchronize in real-time, allowing you to browse, transfer book ownerships, comment, like reviews, and share deep literary analysis together.
