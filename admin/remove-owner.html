Remove Owner Field from Books

This developer utility tool safely and permanently deletes the legacy owner field from all book documents in your Firebase Firestore database. The app's rules and UI have been upgraded to determine ownership dynamically from userId.

Execution Steps

  1. Authentication: Sign in with Google to establish your credentials. You must be authenticated to perform Firestore updates on your owned/shared books.
  2. Audit Search: Scan the database for books that contain the legacy field.
  3. Batch Deletion: Confirm and execute batch writes to permanently remove the field.
